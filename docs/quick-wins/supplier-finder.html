Quick win 1 of 28

A supplier finder with no dead ends

Today

The finder holds 112 supplier names, many of them companies that have left the market. GnErgy sends people to bulb.co.uk, which is now a parked domain. Five others go to a Shell Energy page that didn't load when we tried, even though the same list says Shell Energy customers are now with Octopus.

The change

Point every former supplier straight at the company that now looks after its customers, with one line saying so, and check every link automatically each week.

Your shortlist

See the difference

BehaviourSwitch between today and the change, or see both together. "Today" is a simplified drawing of the live site, not a screenshot. Choosing a view and adding to the shortlist are tracked as events.
