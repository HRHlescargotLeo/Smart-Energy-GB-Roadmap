Quick win 3 of 28

"Do I already have a smart meter?"

Today

The guide links to "Not sure if you already have a smart meter? We can help", which leads to a page of text describing meter types.

The change

A picture-based checker. People choose the meter that looks most like theirs and get one clear answer and the right next step.

Your shortlist

See the difference

BehaviourSwitch between today and the change, or see both together. "Today" is a simplified drawing of the live site, not a screenshot. Choosing a view and adding to the shortlist are tracked as events.

Today

Not sure if you already have a smart meter? We can help

[A page of text explaining the differences between smart meters, first-generation smart meters, prepayment meters and traditional meters, with no way to say which one you have.]

With the change

Choose a meter above to see what it means for you.

Why it's worth doing

People recognise their meter by how it looks, not by its name. A clear answer also catches older smart meters due a switch up and radio teleswitch meters that need replacing.

Also in the big ideasA fuller version of this is part of prototype 1, Get a smart meter. This quick win can be done on its own, without the rest of that prototype.
