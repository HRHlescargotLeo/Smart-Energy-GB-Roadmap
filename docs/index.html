Seven ideas for smartenergygb.org

Clickable prototypes of seven improvements to the Smart Energy GB website. They now carry the Smart Energy GB look, from the live site's own colours, type and photography, so you can see how each one would sit on smartenergygb.org.

Version 3 28 September 2026 Designed prototypes Sample data where marked

The prototypes

New in version 3

We've taken in ideas from the ClerksWell 2027 roadmap proposal for Smart Energy GB. The installation journey, guided assistant, personalised search, real user stories and education through interaction now appear in the prototypes, and every key interaction is tagged as a GA4 event (switch Notes on to see the event log). The proposal's back-office ideas (PDF to page, AI metadata, reporting agents) sit in Umbraco rather than on the public site, so they aren't shown here.

  1. Get a smart meter
    1

    Get a smart meter, from booking to the weeks after

    An installation journey in three stages (before you book, on the day and after installation) with a checklist to tick off, a tidied supplier finder that sends former suppliers' customers to the right place, and a reminder for people who aren't ready to book. Includes a "which meter do I have?" checker.

    Try: type "bulb" in the supplier finder, then tick a few items and watch the progress bar.

    Open prototype
  2. Is my meter working?
    2

    Is my smart meter working?

    A guided checker in place of a long troubleshooting page. Two or three questions lead to one clear answer, covering blank displays, estimated bills, the 4G switch up and old radio teleswitch meters.

    Try: choose "My bills are estimated", answer the questions, then pick a supplier.

    Open prototype
  3. Energy prices
    3

    Energy prices, always current

    One price cap record feeds every figure on the site and switches itself over when the cap changes. With a simpler bill estimator and a peak times clock.

    Try: change the preview date to 1 October, then move the estimator sliders.

    Open prototype
  4. Homepage
    4

    A homepage that sorts people by task

    Three routes in (get one, got one, something's wrong), a guided assistant of five questions for anyone unsure, and "Picked for you" once they've answered. Then the current campaign, a question box and national figures. Plus a campaign landing template, shown with Switch Up, Stay Smart.

    Try: answer the five questions and watch "Picked for you" appear at the top.

    Open prototype
  5. Find an answer
    5

    Find an answer fast

    Search that answers the question on the results page, with a suggested answer for questions asked in your own words, follow-up questions to try, myths beside the facts and "Did this change your mind?" on each myth.

    Try: search "I rent a flat, am I allowed one", then try a follow-up question.

    Open prototype
  6. Real stories
    6

    Real stories from smart meter owners

    Case studies built around the worries people actually have, tagged for renters, homeowners, older and younger people, with a story template that also produces cuts for social and email.

    Try: filter to renters, open the first story, then share your own.

    Open prototype
  7. Estimated bills
    7

    What does an estimated bill cost?

    An interactive scenario: choose a situation you recognise, move two sliders and watch the gap between what you've paid and what you've used become a catch-up bill. Then see the same months with a smart meter.

    Try: choose "A new baby", then drag the months slider to 12.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: the supplier finder, the checklist, the guided assistant, the checker, the estimator, the peak clock, the bill explainer, search and forms. Links to pages outside these seven go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Smart Energy GB (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Price cap and smart meter figures are Ofgem's and the government's published figures. Supplier contact details, display models and flexibility schemes are placeholders.
  • Photography is Smart Energy GB's own, loaded from smartenergygb.org. Photos beside named owners' quotes are left as placeholders, since we don't have pictures of those people.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
