Five ideas for smartenergygb.org

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Smart Energy GB website. They are deliberately in greyscale, so the conversation stays on what each page does and how it works rather than how it looks.

Version 1 28 September 2026 Greyscale prototypes Sample data where marked

The prototypes

  1. Get a smart meter
    1

    Get a smart meter in three steps

    A tidied supplier finder that sends former suppliers' customers to the right place, a short "what happens next" before handing over, a get-ready checklist and a reminder for people who aren't ready to book. Includes a "which meter do I have?" checker.

    Try: type "bulb" in the supplier finder, then choose Remind me later.

    Open prototype
  2. Is my meter working?
    2

    Is my smart meter working?

    A guided checker in place of a long troubleshooting page. Two or three questions lead to one clear answer, covering blank displays, estimated bills, the 4G switch up and old radio teleswitch meters.

    Try: choose "My bills are estimated", answer the questions, then pick a supplier.

    Open prototype
  3. Energy prices
    3

    Energy prices, always current

    One price cap record feeds every figure on the site and switches itself over when the cap changes. With a simpler bill estimator and a peak times clock.

    Try: change the preview date to 1 October, then move the estimator sliders.

    Open prototype
  4. Homepage
    4

    A homepage that sorts people by task

    Three routes in (get one, got one, something's wrong), the current campaign up front, a question box and national figures that update each quarter. Plus a campaign landing template, shown with Switch Up, Stay Smart.

    Try: choose a supplier in the hero, or search "wifi".

    Open prototype
  5. Find an answer
    5

    Find an answer fast

    Search that answers the question on the results page, from one set of answers used across the site. Browse by topic, see myths beside the facts, and tell us whether an answer helped.

    Try: start typing "switch", pick a suggestion, then answer "Did this help?".

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: the supplier finder, the checker, the estimator, the peak clock, search and forms. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Smart Energy GB (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Price cap and smart meter figures are Ofgem's and the government's published figures. Supplier contact details, display models and flexibility schemes are placeholders.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
