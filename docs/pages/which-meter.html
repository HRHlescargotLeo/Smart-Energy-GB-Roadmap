What we're proposing

A picture-based checker. People pick the meter that looks like theirs and get a plain answer and the right next step, whether that's booking a smart meter, expecting a switch up or replacing an old radio teleswitch meter.

Why

The live guide links to "Not sure if you already have a smart meter? We can help", which leads to a page of text. People recognise their meter by how it looks, not by its name.
