Prototype 1 of 7 · Get a smart meter

What we're proposing

One page that takes people from "I'd like a smart meter" through to the weeks after installation, in three stages: before you book, on the day and after. The supplier finder stays at the heart of the first stage, with a tidied list and a short "here's what happens next" before we hand people over. Each stage has a short checklist people can tick off, and there's a way to be reminded later for anyone who isn't ready to book today. This version takes in the installation journey from the ClerksWell 2027 roadmap proposal.

Why

Getting a first installation is still Smart Energy GB's main job to 2030. Today the Get a smart meter page has around 150 words and six links to other topics, and the actual steps sit on a separate page. The finder itself is well built, but some names lead nowhere: GnErgy goes to bulb.co.uk, which is now a parked domain, and five others go to a Shell Energy page even though the same list says Shell customers are now with Octopus. People who aren't ready to book simply leave, and Smart Energy GB has no way to come back to them.

Supplier list is a sample. Links to suppliers are placeholders.

Get a smart meter

Households and small businesses in England, Scotland and Wales can get a smart meter at no extra cost. Here's what to do before you book, what happens on the day and what to expect afterwards.

      On the day

      What happens when the installer arrives

      About two hours

      For gas and electricity together. Your supply is off for a short time while the meters are changed.

      No extra cost

      There's no charge for the installation or the in-home display, and no extra charge on your bill.

      A trained installer

      Installers follow a code of practice that covers safety and how they treat you in your home.

      1. Check their ID before letting them in. If you're not sure, call your supplier on the number on your bill.

      2. A quick safety check before any work starts.

      3. The old meters come out and the smart meters go in. Anything with a clock may need resetting afterwards.

      4. Ask them to show you what each screen means. They can also give you advice on using less energy.

      5. Your display should start showing what you're using straight away.
