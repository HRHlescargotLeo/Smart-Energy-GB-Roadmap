What we're proposing

One template for every case study: the worry, what happened and what changed, in the person's own words, with a short film, the facts about their home and a link to the answer that deals with their worry. The same interview is cut into square, portrait and landscape versions for social, email and paid media.

Why

A consistent structure makes stories quick to produce and easy to compare, and tagging each one to a group means the right story can be shown in the right place: the renting page, the guided assistant or a campaign aimed at renters.

All copy in square brackets is a placeholder for the real interview.
