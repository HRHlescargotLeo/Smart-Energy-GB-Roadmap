Prototype 2 of 7 · Is my smart meter working?

What we're proposing

Replace the long troubleshooting page with a short guided checker. It asks two or three questions and ends in one clear answer: something to try, a reason not to worry, or who to contact and what to say. The same checker covers the 4G switch up and old radio teleswitch meters, and the page explains what to expect in the first weeks after installation.

Why

Government figures show 3.1 million smart meters working in traditional mode at the end of June 2026, and helping people get these working again is now part of Smart Energy GB's remit. Today the troubleshooting page runs to about 1,600 words and repeats the heading "My smart meter is not working". The RTS page still talks about the switch-off as something that will happen, and the new Switch Up, Stay Smart page isn't linked from here at all.

Answers are drafted from the live FAQs and troubleshooting page, for Smart Energy GB to check.

Is my smart meter working?

Answer a few questions and we'll tell you what's going on and what to do next. It takes about a minute.

Smart meter checker

    BehaviourEach answer moves to the next question; "Start again" and the answer trail let people step back. Every route ends in a result with the supplier's contact details. Try "My supplier wants to replace my meter" or "My heating comes on at the wrong times".

    Open questionCould suppliers give Smart Energy GB a direct contact route for smart meter faults, so the result can show the right number instead of a general helpline?

    The first weeks after your installation

    1. Day 1

      Your meters are fitted

      The installer sets up your in-home display and shows you how it works. Your display should start showing what you're using straight away.

    2. First two weeks

      Your supplier connects

      It can take a couple of weeks for your supplier to start getting readings. If they ask you for a reading during this time, you can send one or wait.

    3. First bill

      Accurate readings

      Once connected, your bills use automatic readings, not estimates.

    4. After a month

      Still estimated?

      If bills are still estimated after a month, run the checker or contact your supplier.

    Switch Up, Stay Smart

    Has your supplier asked to replace your smart meter?

    Some older smart meter equipment uses 2G and 3G mobile networks, which are being switched off. Your supplier will contact you to replace it, at no extra cost, so your meter keeps working.
