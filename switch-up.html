What we're proposing

One template for every campaign: a film or hero image, one message, one action, a short "what happens" sequence, answers to the questions the campaign raises, and a link to the press materials. New campaigns launch by filling in the template rather than building a page.

Why

Campaigns (Einstein, Johnny Vegas, Ross Kemp) currently live as homepage blocks that lead to ordinary content pages. The Switch Up, Stay Smart page was updated on 8 September 2026 but isn't linked from the homepage, the About smart meters section, the FAQs or Get a smart meter, and isn't in the sitemap, so people who get a letter from their supplier and search for it have little chance of finding it.

Switch Up, Stay Smart

All technology evolves. Smart meters too.

Energy suppliers across Great Britain are switching older smart meter equipment for new kit, so your meter stays connected. There's nothing you need to do until your supplier gets in touch.

Video: campaign film, with captions and transcript

Has your energy supplier been in touch about a switch up?

What happens

  1. 1

    Your supplier contacts you

    They'll explain what needs switching: usually the communications hub, and sometimes the meter itself if it's an older model.

  2. 2

    You book a time

    They arrange an appointment that suits you. There's no charge.

  3. 3

    You stay smart

    Automatic readings, accurate bills and access to flexible tariffs carry on as before.

Questions about the switch up
