Prototype 4 of 5 · A homepage that sorts people by task

What we're proposing

A homepage built around the three reasons people arrive: they want a smart meter, they have one and want to use it, or something isn't working. Under those sit the current campaign, a search box, up-to-date national figures and owners' stories. Each campaign gets its own landing page from a reusable template, shown here with Switch Up, Stay Smart.

Why

Today's homepage has six question tiles and six green modules that all look alike, with "Find out more" seven times. The newest campaign, Switch Up, Stay Smart, isn't on it at all, even though government now expects Smart Energy GB to encourage people to accept the 4G upgrade. And some pages still quote "over 60% of homes" when government figures now show 72% of meters are smart or advanced.
