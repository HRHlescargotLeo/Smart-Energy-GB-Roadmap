Prototype 1 of 5 · Get a smart meter

What we're proposing

One page that takes people from "I'd like a smart meter" to a booked installation in three clear steps. The supplier finder stays at the heart of it, but with a tidied list, a short "here's what happens next" before we hand people over to their supplier, a get-ready checklist, and a way to be reminded later for anyone who isn't ready to book today.

Why

Getting a first installation is still Smart Energy GB's main job to 2030. Today the Get a smart meter page has around 150 words and six links to other topics, and the actual steps sit on a separate page. The finder itself is well built, but some names lead nowhere: GnErgy goes to bulb.co.uk, which is now a parked domain, and five others go to a Shell Energy page even though the same list says Shell customers are now with Octopus. People who aren't ready to book simply leave, and Smart Energy GB has no way to come back to them.

Supplier list is a sample. Links to suppliers are placeholders.

Get a smart meter

Households and small businesses in England, Scotland and Wales can get a smart meter at no extra cost. It takes three steps, and the installation itself usually takes about two hours.

BehaviourSwitching to "small business" swaps the supplier list in step 1 for business suppliers and adjusts the wording. It replaces the separate business subdomain as the starting point (idea #7).

    • 2

      Book a date with your supplier

      Your supplier will offer you an appointment. Things worth knowing when you book:

      About two hours

      For gas and electricity together. Your supply is off for a short time while the meter is changed.

      No extra cost

      There's no charge for the installation or the in-home display, and no extra charge on your bill.

      Tell them what you need

      If you need extra help, an accessible display or a particular time, say so when you book.

    • 3

      Let the installer in

      A trained installer from your supplier fits the meters and sets up your in-home display.

      Get-ready checklist

      • Clear a path to your gas and electricity meters
      • Be at home for about two hours
      • Ask to see the installer's ID before letting them in
      • Ask them to show you how the in-home display works
      • Keep the display somewhere you'll see it, such as the kitchen
