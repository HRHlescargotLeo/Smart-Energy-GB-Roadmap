Prototype 7 of 7 · Estimated bills, explained

What we're proposing

An interactive scenario that shows what happens when bills are estimated and you use more than your supplier thinks. People choose a situation they recognise, move two sliders and watch the gap between what they've paid and what they've used grow into a catch-up bill. Then they see the same months with a smart meter. It comes from "Education through interaction" in the ClerksWell 2027 roadmap proposal.

Why

Estimated bills and catch-up charges are one of the most concrete reasons to get a smart meter, and one of the hardest to explain in a paragraph. The live site says smart meters mean "no more estimated bills" without showing what an estimated bill costs you. A scenario people can play with makes the point on its own, and each change is a trackable signal of what worries people most.

Costs use the current Ofgem price cap from the shared price record. Scenarios are illustrative, not predictions for any household.

What does an estimated bill really cost?

Without meter readings, your supplier bills you on a guess. If you use more than they guess, the difference catches up with you later. See how it adds up.

BehaviourEach scenario sets the two sliders to a plausible starting point; people can then move them. Hover or tap the chart to see any month. Every scenario change and slider move is a trackable event, so Smart Energy GB can see which situations people explore most.
