What we're proposing

A home for case studies from real smart meter owners, each built around a worry people actually have and tagged to the groups in the ClerksWell 2027 roadmap proposal: renters, homeowners, older and younger people. Visitors filter to someone like them. Each story uses the same template, with cuts for social and email, so one interview feeds every channel.

Why

The people Smart Energy GB still needs to reach are often sceptical because of what they've heard from friends or read online. Today the site has one-line quotes with a first name and a Facebook icon, and a reviews page. A longer story from someone in the same situation answers the worry in a way a fact sheet can't.

Story outlines are placeholders for real interviews. The short quotes are taken from the live homepage.
