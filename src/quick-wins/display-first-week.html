With the change

  1. Day 1

    Find the "now" screen

    It shows what you're using right now, in pounds and pence.

  2. Day 2

    Watch the kettle

    Boil it and see the number jump. That's what a big appliance looks like.

  3. Day 3

    Check your night-time use

    Look before bed. What's left on is your "always on" use.

  4. Day 5

    Look at the week so far

    Your normal day is the one to beat.

  5. Day 7

    Try one change

    Switch something off at the wall, then check again tomorrow.

Why it's worth doing

The display is what people actually have in their hands. If they don't use it in the first week, they usually don't use it at all.

Good to know: Could also run as a short email series for people who sign up for reminders.
