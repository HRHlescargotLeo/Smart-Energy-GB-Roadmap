Quick win 8 of 28

Correct the price cap figures

Today

The price cap page gives the July to September 2026 cap as £1,862 in its text and £1,663 in its table. Ofgem's figure is £1,663. The footnote uses older typical-use figures (2,700 and 11,500 kWh) than Ofgem's current ones (2,500 and 9,500 kWh).

The change

Correct the figures, add the date they apply from and a line saying where they come from, and check every page that repeats them.
