Quick win 21 of 28

A review cycle for every page

Today

Every page shows when it was last updated, which is good, but the FAQs date from August 2025 and several headings have typos: "is gives you", "availble", "reviews:Learn".

The change

Give each page an owner and a review date in Umbraco, fix the typos, and add an automatic spelling and link check before anything is published.

Your shortlist

See the difference

BehaviourSwitch between today and the change, or see both together. "Today" is a simplified drawing of the live site, not a screenshot. Choosing a view and adding to the shortlist are tracked as events.

  • "The in-home display is gives you daily, weekly and monthly energy updates"
  • "Various grants and schemes are availble to help with energy costs"

With the change

  • "The in-home display gives you daily, weekly and monthly energy updates"
  • "Grants and schemes are available to help with energy costs"
  • "Smart meter reviews: learn about the benefits"
  • FAQs: reviewed [date] · Owner: [team] · Next review [date]

Why it's worth doing

It keeps pages accurate without a big rewrite, and it means the "last updated" date shows a page has actually been checked.
