Quick win 22 of 28

Better search descriptions and link text

Today

Several meta descriptions say "Click here", one page has none, and a homepage answer ends "Click here for more information".

The change

Rewrite descriptions so they answer what people searched for, and change "Click here" links to say where they go.

Why it's worth doing

The description is often the first thing people read in search results. Links that say where they go are also easier for people using screen readers.
