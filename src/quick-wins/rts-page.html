Page last updated on 24th April 2026

With the change

Radio Teleswitch (RTS) meters

The RTS signal has been switched off. If you still have an RTS meter, contact your supplier now.

Without the signal, RTS meters may not switch heating and hot water on and off at the right times, and you may pay more than you should.

  1. Check your bill or call your supplier to find out if you have an RTS meter.
  2. Ask them to replace it with a smart meter. There's no charge.
  3. Until then, check your storage heaters and hot water are coming on when you expect.
