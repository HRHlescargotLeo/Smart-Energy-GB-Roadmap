With the change

Myth Smart meters need your Wi-Fi to work.

Fact No. Smart meters send readings over their own secure network, separate from the internet.

Myth You can't switch supplier with a smart meter.

Fact You can switch as you would with any meter. Your smart meter keeps sending readings to your new supplier.

Why it's worth doing

People who've heard something worrying want a straight answer quickly. One consistent format is easier to read, easier to keep accurate and easier to measure.
