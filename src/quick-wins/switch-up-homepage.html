Switch Up, Stay Smart

Your smart meter might need new kit to stay connected

Some older smart meter equipment uses 2G and 3G networks that are being switched off. If your supplier gets in touch, say yes to the switch up. It's at no extra cost.

Find out about the switch up

Why it's worth doing

Government expects Smart Energy GB to encourage people to accept the 4G upgrade. People who get a letter from their supplier and come looking need to find the page.
