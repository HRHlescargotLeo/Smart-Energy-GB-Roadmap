Quick win 14 of 28

Give owners' quotes more context

Today

Homepage quotes carry a first name and a Facebook icon only.

The change

Add the kind of home or situation each person is in, such as renting, prepay or living alone, and keep only quotes that can be verified.

Your shortlist

See the difference

BehaviourSwitch between today and the change, or see both together. "Today" is a simplified drawing of the live site, not a screenshot. Choosing a view and adding to the shortlist are tracked as events.

"Best thing I ever installed was my smart meter. At 71, I don't have to climb a step ladder to read the meter and send readings."

With the change

"Best thing I ever installed was my smart meter. At 71, I don't have to climb a step ladder to read the meter and send readings."

Joy · [Situation, e.g. lives alone] · [Area]

Why it's worth doing

"Someone like me" is more persuasive than a first name. It also lets the right quote be shown in the right place, such as a renter's quote on the renting page.
