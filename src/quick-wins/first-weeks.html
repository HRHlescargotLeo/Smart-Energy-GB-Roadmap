Explain the first weeks after installation

Today

The FAQs answer "I have a smart meter so why have I received an estimated bill?", but nothing explains what to expect in the weeks after an installation.

A short timeline: installed, connecting, first automatic readings, first accurate bill, and what to do if bills are still estimated after a month.

Today

I have a smart meter so why have I received an estimated bill?

[A general answer in the FAQs, with no timeline for a new installation.]

With the change

  1. Day 1

    Your display comes on

    It shows what you're using now and what it's costing.

  2. First two weeks

    Your supplier connects

    It can take a couple of weeks before they get readings. If they ask for one, you can send it.

  3. First bill

    Real readings

    Once connected, your bills use automatic readings, not estimates.

  4. After a month

    Still estimated?

    Check your smart meter is working, or contact your supplier.

Why it's worth doing

People who've just had a smart meter fitted and then get an estimated bill assume it isn't working. Telling them in advance saves them worry and saves suppliers calls.
