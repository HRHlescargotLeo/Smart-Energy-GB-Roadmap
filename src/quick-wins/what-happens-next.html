Quick win 2 of 28

Say what happens next before handing over

Today

Choosing a supplier sends people straight to that supplier's website. A few names show a short message first; most don't, so people land on a page they weren't expecting with nothing to prepare.

The change

Show a short card first: who they're going to, what the supplier will ask for, and how long the installation takes. Then they continue.

Your shortlist

See the difference

With the change

You're going to Octopus Energy

You'll book on their website. Have these ready:

  • Your account number (it's on your bill or in their app)
  • Two or three dates that suit you
  • Where your meters are, and anything that makes them hard to reach

Installation usually takes about two hours, at no extra cost.

Why it's worth doing

It sets expectations at the moment people are most likely to give up, and the click on "Continue" becomes a measurable hand-off to each supplier.
