Prototype 3 of 5 · Energy prices, always current

What we're proposing

Hold the price cap, unit rates and dates in one record in the CMS and have every page read from it. The page then moves from "current" to "upcoming" on the day the cap changes, without anyone editing it. Alongside it: a simpler bill estimator that uses the same figures, and a clock that shows peak and off-peak times at a glance.

Why

Price cap content is some of the most searched-for on the site, and it changes every three months. On the live page the July to September cap appears as £1,862 in the text and £1,663 in the table (Ofgem's figure is £1,663), and the footnote uses older typical-use figures than Ofgem's current ones. When the same number is typed into several places, one of them will eventually be wrong.

Cap figures are Ofgem's for July to December 2026, to be checked before any real use. Estimator presets other than "typical" are illustrative.
